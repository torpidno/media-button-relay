Privacy Policy — Media Button Relay

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Overview

Media Button Relay is an offline utility app that translates hardware volume button presses from paired external Bluetooth media remotes into standard Android media playback commands (such as Play/Pause, Next Track, or Previous Track).

Data Collection & Sharing

Media Button Relay does not collect, store externally, transmit, or share any personal or usage data.

  • The app does not request the INTERNET permission and makes zero network connections.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Your button mapping preferences and timing settings are stored strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.

AccessibilityService API Usage

Media Button Relay uses Android's AccessibilityService API solely with hardware key-event filtering (flagRequestFilterKeyEvents) for one specific purpose: detecting hardware volume key presses from your external Bluetooth remote and dispatching the media key action you configured.

  • Window content retrieval is disabled (canRetrieveWindowContent="false").
  • The app cannot read screen text, passwords, messages, or notifications.
  • The app does not perform automated taps or UI interactions on your behalf.
  • You can enable or disable the service at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue or contact the developer via the support contact listed on the Google Play Store listing.
